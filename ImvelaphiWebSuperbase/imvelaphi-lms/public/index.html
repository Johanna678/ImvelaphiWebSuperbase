About us

Imvelaphi Technologies

Imvelaphi Technologies stands at the forefront of science, technology and engineering — pioneering robotics, biotechnology and 3D-printing solutions tailored to empower the disabled community, including custom, cost-effective 3D-printed prosthetic limbs with robotic elements.

Hands-on learning for young innovators

We teach real robotics, electronics and IoT skills through practical workshops. Every learner builds working projects and gains confidence in hardware, software and systems thinking.

A real Imvelaphi Technologies workshop session — hands-on electronics and logic-gate training with our students.

One of our 3D-printed, robotics-enabled prosthetic hands, built and wired in the Imvelaphi lab.

Engineering solutions with purpose

Our lab combines prosthetics, robotics and assistive technology to build affordable, real-world devices. The same design mindset powers our training programmes and community impact work.
